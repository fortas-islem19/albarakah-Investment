11Client inputs & exclusions

The client provides

  • Logo, brand colors and visual identity (or order as option)
  • Property data: high-quality photos, descriptions, prices, surfaces, addresses
  • Agency texts: about us, services, team
  • Legal info: RC, NIF, terms & conditions, privacy policy, reservation / cancellation / refund policy
  • Payment merchant account (SATIM / Chargily agreement or Stripe)
  • Preferred domain name
  • One contact person to validate each step

Not included

  • Content writing and translation (available as option)
  • Professional property photography / drone video (option)
  • Paid advertising (Facebook / Google Ads), social-media management
  • Third-party subscription fees (payment, SMS, maps, AI, app stores)
  • Legal drafting of contracts (to be validated by the agency’s lawyer or notary)
