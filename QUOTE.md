## 11. What the Client Must Provide

- Logo, brand colors, and visual identity (or order it as an option)
- Property data: photos (high quality), descriptions, prices, surfaces, addresses
- Agency texts: About us, services, team members
- Legal information: company registration (RC, NIF), terms & conditions (CGV), privacy policy, reservation/cancellation & refund policy
- Payment gateway merchant account (bank agreement with SATIM / Chargily, or Stripe account)
- Domain name preference
- One contact person to validate each step

## 12. Exclusions

- Content writing and translation (available as option)
- Professional property photography / drone video (available as option)
- Paid advertising (Facebook / Google Ads) and social-media management
- Third-party subscription fees (payment, SMS, maps, AI, app stores)
- Legal drafting of contracts (to be validated by the agency's lawyer / notary)

---

## 13. Recommendation

- **Budget-limited / just starting:** Offer **A**, then upgrade to B later (the code base is reusable).
- **Best value for most agencies:** Offer **B** — it covers visit booking and reservation requests, which is what clients need first; payment of the deposit can stay offline (at the agency).
- **If the agency wants clients to reserve and pay a deposit online:** Offer **C**.
- **If Albarakah Investment also runs new-build projects (promotion immobilière) and wants a mobile app:** Offer **D**.

A progressive approach is recommended: **launch B in ~6 weeks, then add C modules (payment, CRM, PDF contracts) in phase 2** after the first client feedback.
