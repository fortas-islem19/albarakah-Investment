**What this means for the pitch:**
- They sell **units of one building**, probably **on plan (VSP)**. They need **availability, reservations, installments and trust**, not a generic property-listing site.
- They have **3 products to sell**: homes, **commercial spaces** (B2B), and **parking**.
- Luxury positioning means the website must **look as premium as the catalogue**. The quote already uses their colors, fonts and tower photo, so point that out.
- Their catalogue PDF is **~23 MB**, too heavy for WhatsApp. That's a concrete problem you solve on day one.

## 2. Which plan to push

| Situation | Recommend | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Default | **Plan 3 — Business ($2,500)** | Interactive tower, parking, commercial and CRM: everything a developer actually needs to sell |
| Budget is tight | **Plan 2 — Starter ($1,200)** | Unit pages + availability + reservation requests; upgrade later |
| “Just something online fast” | **Plan 1 — Essential ($600)** | Foot in the door. Use the **upgrade credit** so they move up later |
| They want online deposits / diaspora buyers | **Plan 4 — Professional ($5,000)** | Online payment, buyer portal, construction updates |
| Big group with several projects coming | **Plan 5 — Premium ($10,000)** | Mobile app + multi-project platform |

**Presentation order:** show all 5 plans and spend most of the time on **Plan 3**. Plan 5 is there as the **price anchor**, so that after seeing $10,000, $2,500 feels reasonable.

## 3. Before the meeting (what wins deals)

1. **Build a free demo homepage** in their identity (navy / cream / gold, tower photo, apartments, location). Show it **on your phone**. Seeing their own project online convinces a client far more than any quote. *(Ask Claude to build it.)*
2. **Check domain availability**: `mostalife.com`, `mostalife.dz`, `residence-elcharika.com`. Tell them you can register the one they want.
3. **Print the quote** (Print → Save as PDF) and also have it ready to send on WhatsApp.
4. **Prepare 1–2 examples** of your previous work, or of real-estate sites you admire, to show the quality level.
5. **Fill in all the [brackets]** in the quote: your company name, RC, NIF, contact details and the launch-offer deadline.

## 4. Talking points (value, not features)

- **“Your catalogue is 23 MB.** On WhatsApp it's slow or fails. The website shows the same content instantly, on any phone, and tells you who looked at which apartment.”
- **“Every visitor becomes a lead.”** Price on request + catalogue download = name and phone number for each interested person.
- **“Stop answering ‘is the F4 still available?’ all day.”** The availability table / interactive tower answers it 24/7 and creates urgency (“only 2 F4B left”).
- **“The diaspora buys sea-view apartments.”** French + English pages, WhatsApp, video-call visits, online deposit.
- **“Your 1,213 m² of shops is a separate market.”** A dedicated page targets brands, franchises and investors.
- **“The website pays for itself with one sale.”** Compare the price of the plan to the price of a single apartment.
- **“Facebook ads without a website waste money.”** With Meta Pixel you can measure and retarget the people who looked at a unit.
- **“It's ready for your next projects.”** The same platform hosts future MOSTALIFE residences.

## 5. Questions to ask (discovery)

Ask these before talking prices, and write the answers down. They will confirm which plan fits.

1. How many units in total (apartments per floor, number of floors)? How many are already sold?
2. Are prices public, or “on request”?
3. Do you sell on plan (VSP)? What is the payment schedule (down payment, installments)?
4. How do buyers find you today: Facebook, Instagram, walk-ins, agencies, word of mouth?
5. How many people are in the sales team? How do you track leads today (Excel, notebook, WhatsApp)?
6. What share of buyers are from the diaspora?
7. Do you plan to sell or rent the commercial spaces? To whom?
8. Do you want buyers to pay a deposit online? Do you have a CIB / Edahabia merchant account?
9. When is the official sales launch, and when is delivery?
10. Other projects planned after El Charika?
11. Who decides, and what is the budget range you had in mind?

## 6. Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| **“It's too expensive.”** | “Start with Starter or Essential. 100 % of what you pay counts toward a higher plan within 6 months, so you lose nothing.” *Never lower the price without removing features.* |
| **“We already have a Facebook page.”** | “Facebook is where people discover you. The website is where they decide. It captures their contact, shows availability, and lets you measure which ads bring buyers.” |
| **“We'll do it later.”** | “The best time is before or at the sales launch. Every week without it, leads go uncaptured. The launch offer ends on [date].” |
| **“Our nephew / another freelancer can do it cheaper.”** | “Compare what is included: a page for each unit, availability, CRM, PDF reservations, maintenance and warranty. I've also already designed it in your identity (show the demo).” |
| **“We don't need online payment.”** | “Agreed. Plan 3 is enough: reservation requests online, deposit paid at the office. We can add payment later.” |
| **“How long will it take?”** | “Essential: 1–2 weeks. Business: about 6 weeks. We can launch Essential first while building the rest.” |
| **“What if something breaks?”** | “Free warranty after launch (up to 6 months), then a maintenance plan from $20 / month.” |

## 7. Closing levers

- **Launch offer with a deadline** (already in the quote): free domain + hosting year 1, 1 free month of maintenance, light WhatsApp catalogue, upgrade credit.
- **Phased start:** sign Plan 3 but **go live with the Essential landing page in week 1–2**, so they see results immediately.
- **Payment schedule:** 30 / 30 / 30 / 10 lowers the first payment.
- **Ask for the decision:** “Shall we start with the Business plan? I can begin as soon as I receive the deposit and your list of units.”

## 10. Protect yourself (contract checklist)

- [ ] **Deposit received before starting any work** (50 % or 30 % depending on the plan)
- [ ] Scope = the feature list of the chosen plan; anything else is a paid change ($15 / hour)
- [ ] **Number of design revisions** fixed (2 rounds)
- [ ] **Content deadline:** if the client delivers content late, the timeline moves accordingly
- [ ] Exchange rate USD → DZD fixed in writing on signature
- [ ] Domain registered **in the client's name**; you keep admin access while under maintenance
- [ ] Source code / website transferred **after full payment**
- [ ] Third-party fees (payment gateway, SMS, ads budget, app stores) paid by the client
- [ ] Signed “Bon pour accord” page of the quote with company stamp
